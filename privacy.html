Privacy Policy

Hybrik WP · Last updated 9 September 2026

Hybrik WP is a personal hybrid-training app. This policy explains what the app does with your data. It is written for a single, plain purpose: you should know exactly where your information goes.

What the app stores

  • Your training progress: completed sets, weights, runs and meals.
  • Health data you choose to connect from your Oura account: readiness score, sleep duration, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, body temperature deviation and steps.

Where it is stored

All of it stays on the device you use the app on. There is no Hybrik WP account, no server that receives your training log, and no analytics or advertising code in the app. Nothing is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

How Oura data is used

If you connect your Oura account, the app requests read access to your daily readiness, sleep, activity and heart-rate data. It uses that data for one thing: adjusting the intensity of the training day shown to you, and displaying your recent trend. The data is not copied anywhere else.

Removing access and data

  • Revoke the app's access at any time in your Oura account settings.
  • Delete everything the app holds with "Alle Daten löschen" in the app's Setup screen.
  • Removing the app from your device removes the stored data with it.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
